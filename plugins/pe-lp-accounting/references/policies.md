# Accounting policy defaults (IFRS)

These defaults apply unless the entity's `/policy-setup` record overrides them.
Every output that depends on a policy must state the policy applied.

| # | Area | Default | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Classification | LP interests measured at fair value through profit or loss (FVTPL). Fund units typically fail SPPI; the FVOCI equity election is not available because puttable fund units do not meet the IAS 32 definition of equity (the IAS 32.16A exception does not qualify). | IFRS 9 4.1.4, 5.7.5 |
| P2 | Recognition of calls | Recognise the investment when the contribution is paid. Accrue a payable for a call due on or before period end but unpaid. Undrawn commitment is off balance sheet and disclosed. | IFRS 9; IFRS 7 / IFRS 12 disclosure |
| P3 | Fees and expenses within calls | Part of the contribution (capitalised); captured by fair value remeasurement. Not expensed by the LP. | FVTPL measurement |
| P4 | Equalisation interest | Paid: added to cost. Received: return of capital unless the GP designates it as income. | Substance; market practice |
| P5 | Distributions | Classified per GP notice: return of capital, realised gain, dividend / interest income, WHT. Carrying amount reduced by cash received; fair value remeasured at period end. | IFRS 9 |
| P6 | Presentation | Single line "Net gain / (loss) on financial assets at FVTPL"; realised, unrealised and income split in the notes. | IFRS 7.20(a)(i); IAS 1 / IFRS 18 |
| P7 | Valuation | Latest GP NAV rolled forward for cash flows, FX and known material events; market-proxy adjustment only where the lag covers a material market move; documented; Level 3. | IFRS 13; IPEV Valuation Guidelines |
| P8 | Audited fund FS differences | Change in accounting estimate in the period identified; no restatement unless an error. | IAS 8 |
| P9 | Foreign currency | Non-monetary item at fair value: translated at the rate on the date fair value is measured; FX within fair value movement, analysed in notes. | IAS 21.23(c), 21.30 |
| P10 | Cash flow statement | Investing activities; operating activities where investing is the entity's main business. | IAS 7.14-16 |
| P11 | IFRS 18 (from 1 Jan 2027) | Investing category, or operating where investing in assets is a main business activity; comparatives restated. | IFRS 18 |
| P12 | Control / influence | Assess annually; associates held by venture-capital-type organisations may be measured at FVTPL by election. | IFRS 10; IAS 28.18 |
| P13 | Structured entities | Each fund assessed as an unconsolidated structured entity; maximum exposure = carrying amount + unfunded commitment. | IFRS 12.24-31 |
| P14 | Data standard | ILPA Reporting Template (updated 2025) field definitions; ILPA Performance Template where provided. | Industry standard |
| P15 | Performance | Net IRR (XIRR, adjusted NAV as terminal value), DPI, RVPI, TVPI, KS-PME; gross MOIC as reported by GP. | ILPA / market practice |
| P16 | Thresholds | Fee variance 1% or fixed amount; NAV staleness 1 quarter; break escalation 60 days. | Configurable |

Paragraph references should be confirmed against the current standards on https://www.ifrs.org before external use.
